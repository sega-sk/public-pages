FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. BeatLife works without sign-in.

How do I use HealthKit?
On the Calculator screen tap “HealthKit” and allow heart-rate access. It's optional — you can always type values manually.

How do I delete my data?
Settings > Clear all local data, or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Is this medical advice?
No. BeatLife is for educational and entertainment purposes only.
