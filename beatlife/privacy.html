BeatLife Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

BeatLife respects your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data Collection

BeatLife does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers. Calculations and profile data are stored locally on your device.

Health Data (iOS)

When you grant HealthKit access, BeatLife reads heart-rate data directly from the Health app on your device. This data is not sent to any server, is not used for advertising, and is used only to calculate your estimated life expectancy. BeatLife never writes to Apple Health.

Third-Party Services

BeatLife does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. App updates are delivered through Expo's update service, which receives only standard technical information (such as app version and platform) needed to deliver the update.

Children

BeatLife does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time from Settings > Clear all local data. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Not Medical Advice

BeatLife is for educational and entertainment purposes only. Its estimates are not medical advice.
